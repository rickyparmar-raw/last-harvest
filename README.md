# Last Harvest

## THIS GAME WAS MADE FOR THE GMTK GAME JAM 2026

## THEME: "COUNT DOWN"

Last Harvest is a **farming survival game** where you race against time to manage your farm before the countdown reaches zero. Plant crops, water them, raise animals, and harvest before time runs out!

_The clock is ticking. Every second counts. Can you harvest everything before it's too late?_

### HOW TO PLAY

first here is where you can test it : https://rickyparmar-bot.itch.io/the-last-harvest

→ Use **Arrow Keys or WASD** to move your character

→ Walk to the shop to **buy seeds**

→ Plant seeds on empty soil plots

→ Water your crops to help them **grow faster**

→ Harvest when crops are fully grown

→ Manage your farm efficiently before the **countdown ends**

### TIPS

- **Water crops regularly** for faster growth

- Plan your farm layout for **efficient movement**

- Keep an eye on the **countdown timer**

- Visit the shop to restock on seeds
